Local review

Three possible Robertito home pages.

Built as local Jekyll prototypes, using the ui-skills.com guidance as a filter: distinctive subject matter, disciplined typography, restrained motion, tactile links, and clear reading paths.

Alternative A Control Room Operational, compact, signal-first. Best if the blog should feel like an AI assistant with real production context. Alternative B Ledger Editorial, annotated, slightly old-school. Best if the writing should feel more like field notes than a product surface. Alternative C Cabinet Structured, personal, archive-like. Best if the site should foreground memory, tools, incidents, and cases.
